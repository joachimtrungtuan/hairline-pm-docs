## Flow Overview

This batch tests how Hairline finance staff settle money with providers and affiliates, and how they investigate and prove every money movement afterwards.

- **Provider payouts (FR-017 › Main Flow).** A daily job creates a payout statement for each active provider a few days before its payout date, in status "Pending Approval". A finance admin opens Provider Billing → Upcoming Payments, reviews the statement in the Payout Detail modal, and approves it after re-entering their password. On payout day, approved statements move to "Processing"; statements nobody approved become "Overdue" and can still be approved (immediate transfer). When Stripe confirms the transfer the statement becomes "Paid"; if Stripe rejects it the statement becomes "Failed" and can be retried. Admins can also approve in bulk (Alternative Flow A1), unapprove before payout day, void a statement, and add notes.
- **Reconciliation inputs.** Refunds made after a provider was already paid are deducted from the next payout and carried forward if they exceed it. A provider dispute about a missing booking is investigated in Transaction Search and resolved in the Dispute Resolution Panel (Alternative Flow B1). A currency fluctuation alert is reviewed and decided in the Currency Alert modal (Alternative Flow B2); locked rates never change.
- **Affiliate payouts.** FR-018 calculates monthly affiliate commission; A-05 executes the transfer from Affiliate Billing — Commission Payouts (FR-017 Screen 6).
- **Proof.** The Revenue Dashboard must show live figures with no placeholder or fallback numbers, and every money-moving or status-changing action must appear in the Audit Log with admin, before and after values, and IP address.

Basic invoice, refund-tier, and override controls were tested in Phase 2 (A-05 minimum controls) and are not repeated here. Provider-side results of these actions are checked in B01.
