## Flow Overview

Before a provider can quote in the pilot, it needs a credible public profile, its treatment prices and any add-on packages. This batch covers only the PR-06 functions Phase 1 needs for quoting. The provider-side catalog set up here is what the provider later picks from when building a quote in B05.

- **Profile Management (FR-032 › Profile Management, Settings & Support > Provider profile).** The provider edits clinic details, images, description and contact details (Tab 1), and manages languages (Tab 2), awards (Tab 4) and documents (Tab 6). Validation messages, unsaved-change protection and admin-uploaded document protection are checked.
- **Treatment pricing (FR-024 › Screen 3: Treatment Pricing).** Under Products & Services > Treatments, the provider sets its base price, per-graft price and graft range for each admin-defined treatment, and switches it to Offered.
- **Packages (FR-024 › Screens 4–5: Package Catalog and Package form).** Under Products & Services > Packages, the provider creates, edits (which creates a new version), clones and deactivates add-on packages.
- **Access and isolation (FR-031 Provider Settings permissions; FR-024 privacy rule; FR-032 › Rule 6).** Catalogs are private to each provider. Team members without Edit Profile or Manage Treatments cannot change them, and a suspended provider sees everything as view-only.

Account settings, notification preferences, Help Centre, support cases, reviews and billing settings are not in Phase 1 (see plan §1.2).
