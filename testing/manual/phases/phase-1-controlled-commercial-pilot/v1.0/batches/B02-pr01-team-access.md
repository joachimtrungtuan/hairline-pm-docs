## Flow Overview

Once a clinic is onboarded (B01), its owner builds a team and controls what each person can do. This batch tests that flow end to end on the provider dashboard. It also checks the Phase 1 exit-gate controls: live role-based access, session revocation and provider isolation.

- **Sign-in.** Owners and team members log in to the provider dashboard. Repeated failed attempts lock the account for a period (FR-026: 5 attempts, 15 minutes by default).
- **Invite a team member (FR-009 › Main Flow: Invite Team Member; Screens 1–2).** From the "Team" tab, the owner fills in the invitation form and clicks "Send Invitation". The invitee gets a link valid for 7 days, accepts it on the Invitation Landing page, creates an account and completes the First-Login Permission Tour (Screens 7–9). Duplicate, cross-provider, expired and already-used invitations are handled by the alternative and error flows.
- **Change a role (FR-009 › Main Flow: Change Team Member Role; Screen 3).** The new role's permissions apply on the member's next page load, even during an active session. The primary Owner role can never be changed.
- **Remove or suspend a member (FR-009 › Main Flow: Remove Team Member; Screen 4).** Removal revokes the member's sessions immediately, and a removed member who tries to log in is told they no longer have access.
- **Permissions and isolation (FR-031 provider permission matrix; FR-009 › Data & Privacy Rules).** Roles without team permissions cannot reach team functions, and one provider can never see another provider's team or emails.

- {S1} is an Active team member of {V1} with a non-Owner, non-Manager role based on Clinical Staff (it has no View Team Members or View Inquiries permission under FR-031). If {S1}'s actual role differs, record it in Notes for PR01-FR009-16 and PR01-FR009-17.
- {V1} has used fewer seats than its Seat Limit, except where a case changes this.
- The team controls fresh mailboxes {Invite mailbox 2} and {Invite mailbox 3}, which have never been used on Hairline. Their addresses are shared privately and are not written here.
- Two browsers or one normal and one private window are available, so that two sessions can be open at the same time.
- Login lockout settings are at the FR-026 defaults (5 attempts, 15 minutes), unless B04 has changed them. Record the values in use in Notes.
