## Flow Overview

This batch covers what a clinic does with a patient inquiry from the moment it is distributed until its quote reaches a final state.

1. **Receive and review (FR-003 › Workflow 2, Workflow 3; Screens 9–10).** Hairline sends each inquiry only to eligible providers in the patient's chosen countries or providers the patient selected. The clinic sees an anonymised inquiry list and detail: masked name, no phone or email, read-only medical answers and a colour-coded medical alert. Another clinic's inquiry must never be visible. Search and filters follow FR-022 › PR-02 Screen 9.
2. **Author and send a quote (FR-004 › Workflow 1).** The clinic builds a parent quote across seven tabs: Treatment Service, Package Options (1–5 options from its own package library), Grafts & Visual Plan, Dates & Pricing, Option Treatment Plans, Clinical Details & Notes, and Review & Submit. Validation covers required fields, positive price, appointment slots within the offered date range and in the future, and attachment limits. The quote expiry and currency are fixed at first send from the FR-026 Quote Configuration.
3. **Edit and lifecycle (FR-004 › Workflows 2–4).** A sent quote can be edited before expiry, which creates a new version without moving the expiry. Expired, accepted and cancelled quotes are read-only. When the patient cancels the inquiry or accepts another clinic's quote (setup states prepared by the team), the clinic's quotes change state, rows grey out and actions are disabled. Patient identity stays masked until payment, even after acceptance.
4. **Team permissions (FR-031 › A4).** A team member whose provider role lacks Respond to Inquiries can view inquiries but cannot create or send quotes.

B06 checks the same inquiries and quotes from the admin side, so keep the inquiry and quote IDs used here in **Notes**.

  - INQ-2: submitted by {P2}, Country A, {V1} explicitly selected, one Standard Yes answer (amber alert).
  - INQ-3: submitted by {P3}, Country B, distributed only to {V3}, no Yes answers (green).
  - INQ-4: submitted by {P4}, Country A, distributed to {V1}; the team cancels it during PR02-FR004-10 when asked.
  - INQ-5: submitted by {P5}, Country A; {V1} and {V2} have each sent a quote; the team has accepted {V2}'s quote as {P5}. No payment has been made.
  - INQ-6: submitted by {P6}, Country A; {V1} has a sent quote whose expiry has already passed.
  - INQ-7: submitted by {P7}, Country A, distributed to {V1}, no quotes yet.
- No payment has been confirmed on any of these inquiries, so every patient identity must stay masked.
