## What changed in the PRD

File: `local-docs/project-requirements/functional-requirements/fr011-aftercare-recovery-management/prd.md`

- **Provider access to patient details**: the assigned provider sees patient details from assignment in the standalone flow and from payment in the post-treatment add-on flow, where assignment follows payment; the admin exemption stays. The four lines that said "only after payment" are reworded (REQ-011-010).
- **Milestones and progress**: a milestone closes when the scan, questionnaire and education tasks are done. Medication tick-offs are adherence tracking only: they do not block a milestone and are left out of the progress percentage (REQ-011-007, REQ-011-023, REQ-011-024, REQ-011-027). Owner default 288.
- **Setup and activation (Workflow 1)**: setup starts on the FR-010 completion signal; FR-006 owns the move of the booking to Aftercare. The plan activates when the provider confirms it (REQ-011-055) or an admin activates it (REQ-011-019). New alternative flows: stalled setup (reminder after 2 days, escalation after 4 days, both admin settings, then reassign or manual activation with a mandatory reason and an audit entry; default 289), early close by an admin decision, and a repeated completion signal that returns the existing plan (REQ-011-017, REQ-011-090, REQ-011-091). The retry of a failed signal stays in FR-010 (REQ-010-086).
- **Coordinator**: the Aftercare Coordinator works on admin Screens 13 to 19 (plan configuration, scan review, escalations) when the Hairline team delivers; the specialist screens and the coordinator screens are tied to their actors (REQ-011-004).
- **Who is paid**: the provider who delivers is paid the price less commission; the Hairline team delivering by default or after a reassignment keeps the full price with no commission line; after a provider handover the provider gets a fixed handover fee set by the admin (default 0). Commission is a percentage of the aftercare price (REQ-011-040, REQ-011-057). Owner default 290.
- **Request statuses and timers (Workflows 2 and 2b)**: new statuses Payment due and Expired; the 48 hours run from the payment-due notice (standalone) or from the failed payment (add-on); the 7-day unassigned timer counts from submission, pauses while the request is Awaiting provider availability, and in the add-on flow counts from payment with a full refund on expiry; a due-time field is added (REQ-011-037, REQ-011-049, REQ-011-050, REQ-011-052, REQ-011-053). Owner default 291.
- **Final template**: the provider may pick an alternative only among templates offering the pricing option the patient chose; Screen 7 shows the final template; when the price differs the patient accepts it or cancels at no charge (request Rejected with the system reason "cancelled by patient") (REQ-011-100). Owner default 292.
- **Add-on and entry sources**: no add-on while a plan for the same booking is Active or Completed (REQ-011-092); a third entry source, conversion from FR-038 monitoring, with source, monitoring case, scan or photo set, report file and idempotency key; a repeated key returns the existing request; refused after the monitoring case is completed; a failed or abandoned request returns the patient to FR-038 Screen 6 with the draft intact (REQ-011-093, REQ-011-094, REQ-038-068).
- **Scan task**: uses the FR-002 media-only call with at least one photo and the scan id as idempotency key; staged photos never attached to a scan are removed after 7 days (REQ-011-095, REQ-002-047, REQ-002-051).
- **Plan completion and cancellation**: the plan completes at the end of the last milestone or the subscription term (whichever is later), stops all reminders including REQ-011-088 and emits a completion event that FR-006 reads (REQ-011-096). A patient can cancel from Screen 7: a monthly subscription runs to the end of the paid month with no refund; a plan paid in full stops at the end of the current month with a pro-rata refund of unused full months; commission and payout follow in proportion (REQ-011-097, REQ-011-083). Owner default 293.
- **Extensions and major changes**: proposed by the provider (or coordinator) with a reason, approved or rejected by the admin (Proposed, Approved, Rejected), the patient is notified, and extra cost needs the patient's acceptance and payment; a rejection leaves the plan unchanged (REQ-011-098, REQ-011-020, REQ-011-067). Owner default 294.
- **Urgent flag**: the plan carries an urgent flag and its raised-at time; the 2 hours count from raised-at; the assigned provider or coordinator clears it with a note; with no response the case escalates to the admin; dashboards count flagged plans (REQ-011-099, REQ-011-029).
- **Questionnaire sets**: Screen 16 surfaces Aftercare sets plus every Multi-Context set; a set version is stored on each response; set assignment and replacement are audited (REQ-011-071).
- **Smaller corrections**: the Screen 8 slip on the medication adherence rule now points to Screen 10; the 48-hour escalation of an overdue task is stated as counted from the task due date (REQ-011-025); Dependencies name FR-006 and FR-038 and the two new FR-002 rules; new summary lines REQ-011-090 to REQ-011-100; the change log row and Last Updated are set.
