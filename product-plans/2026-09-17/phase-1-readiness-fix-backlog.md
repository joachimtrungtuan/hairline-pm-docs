## 1.2 User Stories In Scope

### Provider

- As a provider team member, I can log in, act within my role, and my access is revoked when suspended or my role changes.
- As a provider, I receive anonymized inquiries, review questionnaire and scan data required for quoting, and submit a tailored parent quote with package options and scheduled dates.
- As a provider, I maintain the clinic profile, treatment pricing, and package catalog needed to author valid quotes.
- As a provider, I can withdraw or archive an unaccepted quote with a recorded reason.

### Admin

- As an admin, I onboard pilot providers through the wizard, activate them, and manage status with audit logging.
- As an admin, I oversee distributed inquiries and quotes using only the documented oversight actions, with audit on every action.
- As an admin, I configure access, the treatment catalogue, and the active inquiry questionnaire set that drives inquiry intake.

### Shared Services / Platform Foundations

- A valid patient inquiry enters through an agreed existing ingress channel; the channel itself is proven, not assumed.
- Production Phase 1 data (approved catalogue, packages, prices, profiles, cohort configuration) is loaded and visible; production fails visibly when data is missing.
