What do we need to agree before Phase 1 begins?

1Who supplies and approves the production catalogue: treatment services, packages, prices, provider profiles, and availability?
2Which providers form the pilot cohort, and who owns onboarding their organisation, team accounts, and first inquiry-to-quote journey?
3What feedback loop do pilot providers use: test sessions, in-product feedback, weekly review, and a named response owner?
4How do we name and position the launch: provider beta, controlled public onboarding, or another staged rollout model?
5What evidence moves a capability from pilot access to broader public onboarding: usage, stakeholder acceptance, provider feedback, or operational sign-off?
